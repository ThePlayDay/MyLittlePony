# Pony Cards 2.49 — achievements, collection tools and pack pity

- Added 15 server-tracked achievements with progress and one-time coin rewards.
- Added collection filters (all / owned / favorites), rarity/name sorting, and persistent favorites.
- Added distinct epic and legendary pack reveal animations and reveal sounds.
- Added independent legendary pity counters per pack: basic 25, magic 15, royal 7.
- Added persistent counters for columns, pack openings, collected coins, boss wins, no-damage boss clears, and rarity pulls.
- Added no-damage tracking to run completion requests.

## Pack economy

Pack prices are 60 / 110 / 240 coins (basic / magic / royal). A guaranteed legendary is reached after at most 25 / 15 / 7 openings respectively. Maximum spend to reach the guarantee is therefore 1,500 / 1,650 / 1,680 coins. At the listed natural legendary odds (1% / 3% / 8%), the expected spend is also close across tiers; higher-tier packs give better immediate odds without making the basic pack the dominant bargain.

Pity counters are independent for each pack and reset when that pack awards any legendary card, whether naturally or through pity.
